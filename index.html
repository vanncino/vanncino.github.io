Featured venture

Nexus Point

One app for shopping, supply, delivery and rides.

The problem

Buying goods, finding suppliers, arranging delivery and booking a ride usually means several apps, phone calls and middlemen. Small sellers and transport providers lose time and customers.

The solution

A single platform where four groups meet: customers who order, sellers who list products, suppliers who restock sellers, and transport providers who deliver goods and people.

How it earns

A small commission on each completed order, delivery and ride, paid through mobile money.

Where it is

Full requirements specification complete (v1.0, September 2026). Next: building the first working version.

What investment unlocks

  • A working first version on Android and iOS
  • Mobile money payments and automatic payouts
  • Cloud hosting that scales with real users
  • A pilot launch in a first city
